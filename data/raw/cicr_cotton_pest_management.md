# ICAR-CICR Cotton Advisory for Maharashtra Farmers (Vidarbha & Khandesh)

## Section 1: Sowing Guidelines, Spacing, and Early Care (Page 1)
Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*) cultivation in Maharashtra begins with the onset of regular monsoon rains in late June or early July. Avoid pre-monsoon dry sowing to prevent sucking pest outbreaks and bollworm synchronization.
- Spacing: For Bt hybrids, maintain 90 cm x 60 cm or 120 cm x 45 cm for high density planting.
- Seed rate: 2-2.5 packets (450 g each) per acre. Bt cotton seeds come pre-treated with insecticide, but non-Bt refuge seeds must be planted as 5 rows around the boundary (or 20% refuge) to delay pest resistance against Cry proteins.

## Section 2: Pink Bollworm (PBW) Management (Page 2)
Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*): The most destructive pest in Maharashtra cotton belts. Larvae enter bolls, feed on developing seeds, and prevent lint development, leading to "rosetted flowers" and stained lint.
- ETL: 8 moths per trap per night for 3 consecutive nights OR 10% rosetted flowers / green boll damage.
- Step-by-Step Window Management:
  - 45-60 Days: Install delta/pheromone traps with Pectino-lures at 2 traps per acre for monitoring. Destroy rosetted flowers manually.
  - 60-90 Days: Install PBW pheromone traps at 8 traps per acre for mass trapping. Release parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* at 60,000 eggs per acre at weekly intervals.
  - 90-120 Days (Chemical Intervention if ETL crossed): Spray Profenofos 50% EC at 2 ml per litre or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG at 0.5 gram per litre. Rotate chemical classes to prevent resistance.

## Section 3: Sucking Pests (Whitefly, Jassids, Thrips, Aphids) (Page 3)
1. Jassids / Leafhopper (*Amrasca biguttula*): Yellowing and curling of leaf margins downwards ("hopper burn"). Spray Flonicamid 50% WDG at 0.3 g per litre or Dinotefuran 20% SG at 0.3 g per litre.
2. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*): Honeydew secretion leads to sooty mould on leaves and lint. Transmits cotton leaf curl virus. Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP at 1.2 g per litre or Pyriproxyfen 10% EC at 2 ml per litre.
3. Natural Enemies Conservation: Avoid broad-spectrum synthetic pyrethroids before 90 days as they kill beneficial predators (ladybird beetles, chrysoperla) and trigger secondary whitefly surges.

## Section 4: Cotton Diseases (Bacterial Blight, Parawilt, Root Rot) (Page 4)
1. Bacterial Blight (*Xanthomonas citri pv. malvacearum*): Angular water-soaked spots on leaves bounded by veins ("angular leaf spot") and black lesions on stems ("black arm").
   - Control: Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP at 2.5 g per litre + Streptocycline at 0.1 g per litre of water.
2. Sudden Wilt / Parawilt: Rapid drooping of leaves after heavy irrigation or intense rainfall following dry spells.
   - Control: Drench affected plants with Copper Oxychloride (2.5 g/L) and 1% Urea solution (10 g/L) around root zone within 24-48 hours of symptoms.
