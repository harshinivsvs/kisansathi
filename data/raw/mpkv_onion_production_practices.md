# MPKV Rahuri Package of Practices for Onion in Maharashtra

## Section 1: Varieties, Nursery and Sowing Calendar (Page 1)
In Maharashtra (Nashik, Ahmednagar, Pune, Solapur belts), onions are grown in three seasons:
1. Kharif: Sowing in nursery May-June, transplanting July-August. Varieties: Baswant 780, Phule Samarth.
2. Late Kharif (Rangada): Sowing August-September, transplanting October-November. Varieties: Phule Samarth, Phule Safed.
3. Rabi: Sowing October-November, transplanting December-January. Varieties: N-2-4-1, Bhima Kiran, Bhima Shakti.
Seed rate: 8-10 kg per hectare. Treat seed with *Azospirillum* and *Phosphorus Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB)* at 25 g/kg seed and Thiram at 2 g/kg seed. Seedlings are ready for transplanting at 45-50 days old.

## Section 2: Fertilizer Dosage and Water Management (Page 2)
Apply 25 tonnes FYM per hectare during ploughing. Recommended NPK dose for Rabi onion is 100 kg Nitrogen, 50 kg Phosphorus, and 50 kg Potash per hectare, plus 30 kg Sulphur per hectare.
Sulphur application is essential to increase pungency, pyruvic acid content, bulb firmness, and storage life.
Apply 50% N, full P, K, and S at transplanting. Apply remaining 50% N in two equal splits: 30 days and 45 days after transplanting. Stop irrigation 15 days before harvest to ensure neck curing and reduce storage rotting.

## Section 3: Onion Thrips and Management (Page 3)
Onion Thrips (*Thrips tabaci*): The single most damaging pest for Maharashtra onion growers. Adults and nymphs lacerate leaf epidermis and suck sap, producing silvery-white blotches. Tips turn brown and curl downwards ("Piliya" symptom).
- Economic Threshold Level (ETL): 15-20 thrips per plant.
- Cultural & Biological Control: Install blue sticky traps at 20-25 per acre. Spray Neem oil (10,000 ppm) at 2 ml per litre or *Verticillium lecanii* (fungal bioagent) at 5 grams per litre.
- Chemical Control: If ETL is crossed, spray Fipronil 5% SC at 1 ml per litre of water or Profenofos 50% EC at 1.5 ml per litre. Always mix a wetting agent/spreader (0.5 ml/L) since onion foliage has a waxy surface. Observe a 14-day pre-harvest interval (PHI).

## Section 4: Purple Blotch and Stemphylium Blight (Page 4)
1. Purple Blotch (*Alternaria porri*): Small water-soaked lesions that develop sunken purple centres with yellow margins, spreading along the length of leaves.
   - Control: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP (2.5 g/L) or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC (1 ml/L). Ensure clean field sanitation and destroy crop debris.
2. Basal Rot (*Fusarium oxysporum*): Yellowing of leaves starting from tips, drooping, and bulb base decays with white pinkish mycelium.
   - Control: Crop rotation with pulses or sorghum for 3 years. Dip seedling roots in Carbendazim 50% WP (1 g/L) solution for 15 minutes before transplanting. Drench field with *Trichoderma viride* enriched FYM.
