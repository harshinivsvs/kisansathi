# ICAR-IISR Soybean Advisory for Maharashtra Farmers (Vidarbha & Marathwada)

## Section 1: Sowing Time, Seed Rate and Inoculation (Page 1)
Soybean (*Glycine max*) is the major oilseed crop of Maharashtra. Sowing must be undertaken only after receiving 75-100 mm of cumulative monsoon rainfall and soil has attained sufficient moisture (usually late June to first week of July).
- Seed rate: 65-70 kg per hectare (for germination rate > 70%). Maintain 45 cm row spacing and 5-7 cm plant-to-plant spacing.
- Seed Treatment Sequence (FIR technique - Fungicide, Insecticide, Rhizobium):
  1. Fungicide: Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS at 2 g/kg seed to protect against seed rot and collar rot.
  2. Insecticide: Thiamethoxam 30% FS at 10 ml/kg seed to prevent stem fly and girdle beetle during initial 20-25 days.
  3. Biofertilizer: *Bradyrhizobium japonicum* culture (5 g/kg seed) + PSB (5 g/kg seed) to fix atmospheric nitrogen.

## Section 2: Weed Management and Nutrient Balance (Page 2)
The critical crop-weed competition period is 15-35 days after sowing.
- Pre-emergence herbicide: Apply Diclosulam 84% WDG at 26 g per hectare or Pendimethalin 38.7% CS at 1.75 litres per hectare within 48 hours of sowing on moist soil.
- Early post-emergence (at 15-20 days): Apply Quizalofop-ethyl 5% EC at 1.0 litre/ha + Chlorimuron-ethyl 25% WP at 36 g/ha for mixed weed flora.
- Fertilizer application: Apply 30 kg N, 60 kg P2O5, 30 kg K2O, and 20 kg Sulphur per hectare as basal dose. Being a legume, excessive chemical nitrogen application suppresses nodulation.

## Section 3: Stem Fly and Girdle Beetle Management (Page 3)
1. Stem Fly (*Melanagromyza sojae*): Maggots bore into stem medullary cavity causing wilting and hollow stems.
   - Management: Seed treatment with Thiamethoxam 30% FS is most effective. At 15-20 days stage, spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (0.3 ml/L) or Indoxacarb 15.8% EC (0.7 ml/L).
2. Girdle Beetle (*Obereopsis brevis*): Female beetle makes two parallel girdles/rings on petioles or stems and lays eggs. Foliage above the girdle wilts and dries.
   - Management: Hand-pick and destroy cut petiole parts showing girdling in early stage. Spray Profenofos 50% EC at 2 ml per litre or Thiacloprid 21.7% SC at 1.5 ml per litre.

## Section 4: Rust and Pod Blight / Anthracnose (Page 4)
1. Soybean Rust (*Phakopsora pachyrhizi*): Pin-sized brown-grey pustules develop on the underside of leaves, causing premature defoliation and severe yield loss in humid weather.
   - Management: Monitor regularly when relative humidity exceeds 85% and temperatures stay around 22-28°C. Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC at 1 ml per litre or Propiconazole 25% EC at 1 ml per litre. Repeat after 15 days if conditions persist.
2. Anthracnose / Pod Blight (*Colletotrichum truncatum*): Dark, sunken lesions on pods and stems; seeds become shrivelled.
   - Management: Spray Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP at 2 g per litre of water at pod initiation stage.
