# ICAR-IIHR Tomato Advisory for Maharashtra Farmers

## Section 1: Soil, Climate, and Nursery Preparation (Page 1)
Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*) grows best in well-drained, sandy loam to clay loam soils with a pH between 6.0 and 7.0. In Maharashtra, Kharif sowing occurs in June-July, and Rabi sowing in October-November. 
For 1 acre of land, use 150-200 grams of hybrid seeds. Treat seeds before sowing with *Trichoderma viride* at 4 grams per kg of seed or Thiram at 2 grams per kg to prevent damping off disease. Nursery beds should be raised 15 cm above ground level. Seedlings are ready for transplanting in 25-30 days after sowing.

## Section 2: Nutrient and Irrigation Management (Page 2)
Apply 20-25 tonnes of well-decomposed FYM (Farm Yard Manure) per hectare during land preparation. The recommended fertilizer dose is 120 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus (P2O5), and 60 kg Potash (K2O) per hectare.
Apply 50% Nitrogen and the entire Phosphorus and Potassium as basal application. Apply the remaining Nitrogen in two split doses at 30 days and 50 days after transplanting. 
Drip irrigation is highly recommended in Maharashtra to conserve water and prevent foliar humidity which promotes fungal infections. Irrigate at 7-8 day intervals during winter and 4-5 day intervals in summer.

## Section 3: Major Diseases and Management (Page 3)
1. Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*): Concentric brown-black rings (target board pattern) appear on lower leaves. 
   - Control: Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2 grams per litre of water or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP at 2.5 grams per litre. Ensure full coverage on the underside of foliage. Always adhere to container label safety precautions.
2. Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV): Transmitted by whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*). Plants show upward curling, puckering, and stunting.
   - Control: Erect yellow sticky traps (15-20 traps per acre) to monitor and catch whiteflies. For biological control, spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 10000 ppm at 2 ml per litre. For severe infestations, spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP at 1 gram per litre. Follow safety waiting periods before harvest.
3. Bacterial Wilt (*Ralstonia solanacearum*): Rapid wilting of the plant without yellowing, vascular browning in the stem.
   - Control: Crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops (maize, sorghum). Drench root zone with Copper Oxychloride (2.5 g/L) + Streptocycline (1 g per 10 L water).

## Section 4: Major Pests and Integrated Pest Management (Page 4)
1. Fruit Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*): Larvae bore circular holes into tomato fruits.
   - Control: Plant African Marigold (*Tagetes erecta*) as a trap crop (1 row of marigold after every 16 rows of tomato). Install pheromone traps at 5 per acre. Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) formulations at 1.5 grams per litre or NPV at 250 LE per acre. If chemical intervention is critical, spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC at 0.3 ml per litre.
2. Tuta absoluta (Pinworm / Leafminer): Mines into leaves leaving blotchy silver-white mines and bores into young fruits.
   - Control: Install pheromone traps with Tuta lures (8-10 per acre). Spray Spinosad 45% SC at 0.3 ml per litre. Wear gloves and mask during spray application.
